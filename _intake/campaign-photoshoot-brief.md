# Malike Restaurant — Full Campaign Photoshoot Brief

**Client:** Malike Restaurant  
**Location context:** Anumansa St, Osu, Accra  
**Phone / WhatsApp:** 054 847 4672

**Purpose:** One full shoot day (or AI generation batch) covering every asset needed for Facebook page setup, 30-day content, stories, cover, and conversion posts.  
**Look:** Hyperrealistic commercial food photography — as if a Ghanaian restaurant hired a photography crew with soft daylight, shallow depth of field, natural textures, no plastic food styling, no fake props logos.

---

## Global shoot direction (apply to every frame)

- **Style:** Hyperrealistic editorial food photography, Canon/Sony full-frame look, 35mm–85mm primes.
- **Light:** Soft morning/afternoon Ghana daylight through a window or open shade; warm key, gentle fill; avoid harsh flash and neon gels.
- **Surfaces:** Clean enamel or ceramic plates, wooden table, banana leaf accents sparingly, modest checkered or plain cloth — local chop-bar elegance, not fine-dining white tablecloth.
- **Hands:** Occasional Ghanaian hands plating or holding a bowl (natural nails, no jewelry clutter).
- **Steam:** Visible light steam on hot banku, fufu, soups, and grilled fish where realistic.
- **Colour grade:** Warm, appetising, slightly contrasty; true food colours (red palm oil glow, golden grill marks, white banku, soft fufu).
- **Do not show:** Fake discounts, invented ratings, competitor brands, watermark text, “AI” artefacts, melted plastic textures, empty generic stock kitchens with no Ghanaian cues.
- **Text overlays:** None burned into photos unless a shot is labelled “graphic base” — keep plates clean for later caption design.
- **Aspect ratios to export:**  
  - Cover: 16:9 landscape (820×312 feel, export high-res)  
  - Profile/logo: 1:1  
  - Feed posts / cards: 1:1 and 4:5  
  - Stories: 9:16
